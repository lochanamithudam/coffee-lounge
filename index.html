Our Story

A Sanctuary for
Coffee Lovers

Nestled in the heart of the city, Coffee Lounge was born from a singular obsession — crafting the perfect cup. Since 2018, we've been sourcing the world's finest single-origin beans, roasted in-house to our exacting standards, and brewed by passionate artisans who treat every pour as a work of art.

Our lounge is more than a café — it's a destination. An intimate sanctuary where the aroma of freshly ground espresso mingles with soft jazz, where conversations flow as freely as our signature blends, and where every detail, from our hand-thrown ceramic cups to our candlelit ambiance, reflects our commitment to the extraordinary.

We partner directly with smallholder farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Indonesia — paying fair prices and building relationships that span generations. Every cup you savor carries the story of the hands that grew it.
